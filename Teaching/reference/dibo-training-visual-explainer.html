DiBO trains a diffusion language model to propose improved designs from a small, fixed set of scored examples. Its key move is to steer generation through training, beyond examples in a prompt.

What to carry forward

DiBO shows a way to train a diffusion generator on a scarce, labeled offline design pool. The paper’s ablations support the sequence of domain adaptation → supervised tuning → reward training, plus explicit design/label delimiters and similarity-selected prompt examples. That is a research direction for structured search, not direct evidence that the method works for Pokémon team building.
