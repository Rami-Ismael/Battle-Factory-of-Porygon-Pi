The trap this figure exists to close

"The hidden state holds a belief about the opponent" is an interpretation of an ordinary vector, not a component you could point at in the code. A Bayes-Adaptive POMDP maintains a posterior you can print — Dirichlet counts φ, ψ, one tally per successor. Metamon has 512–1280 floats that behave as if it did. Same job; only one of them is inspectable. See how a BAPOMDP keeps a posterior.

Consequence for the VGC port: nothing on rungs 1–3 needs inventing, and rung 4 is bought by the observation design. Doubles with team preview reveals more up front than Gen 1–4 OU does, so the memory burden shifts — from "what are the other five" toward "which four did they bring, and what are the sets".
