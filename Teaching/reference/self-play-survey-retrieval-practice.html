Self-play survey — can you still recall it?

Answer out loud or on paper before opening the answer. If a question feels fuzzy, that is the one to re-read.

1 · In one sentence: what is the single design question every self-play method answers?
Which copy do you practice against? Everything else (gates, pruning, priority weights) is detail hanging off that choice.ELI5 §1
2 · A friend says "fictitious play trains you against the average of all your past networks." Two things are wrong with that?
(a) No averaged network is ever built — you pull one whole frozen snapshot per practice session and fight it. (b) The past versions are frozen: they were created after their battles, then never learn again — unlike vanilla self-play's opponent, which is still changing. The "average" only describes the box of snapshots statistically, after many pulls.Lesson 0008 · ELI5 §4
3 · Why can pure vanilla self-play forget?
It only ever fights its newest self. Old weaknesses have no opponent left to expose them, so counter-tricks to old strategies quietly rot.ELI5 §3
4 · What does the survey mean by "uniform over all earlier — Eq. (6)"?
Each practice session, the next opponent is picked with equal probability from every frozen snapshot saved so far — Monday's version is exactly as likely as this morning's. "Earlier" means earlier checkpoints in the box, nothing vaguer than that.Paper note table · ELI5 §4
5 · δ-uniform and PFSP both keep FP's box but change one thing each. What?
δ-uniform: sample uniformly only from the newest slice of history (old enough not to forget, new enough not to waste time). PFSP: sample weighted by a priority function — opponents that still beat you get picked more often.ELI5 §5
6 · What is an MSS (meta-strategy solver), stated as a function?
A function from the performance matrix 𝒫 (who beats whom) to the interaction matrix Σ (how every policy samples its opponents next round). It never plays; it only sets the opponent-selection rule.Paper note §MSS · survey Algo. 1
7 · Match each MSS to its method: uniform-over-history / SOLVE-NASH-on-restricted-game / priority function f(P).
Uniform over history → naive fictitious play. SOLVE-NASH → double oracle (opponent sampled from the Nash strategy of the restricted game). Priority function → prioritized fictitious self-play.Survey Algo. 5–6 · paper note §MSS
8 · Where does the double-oracle convergence guarantee live — and what happens to it when the oracle is PPO?
In the oracle, not the MSS: DO reaches the full game's equilibrium only if the oracle computes an exact best response. With PPO (an approximate-best-response oracle, like every VGC-Bench arm) the guarantee evaporates — claims become empirical only.Record 0002 · lesson 0002
9 · Why does chasing-your-newest-self work in Go but fail in rock-paper-scissors-shaped games? Name the property.
Transitivity. Go is a ladder (if A beats B and B beats C, A beats C), so the newest self really is the hardest opponent so far. RPS is cyclic — strength chases itself in a circle and training runs forever without progress.ELI5 §2, §7 · survey §5.2
10 · Which side of that split is Pokémon VGC on, and what does this project therefore measure instead of one score?
The cyclic side — team matchups form who-beats-whom circles. So the project measures a matchup table (who beats whom) rather than a single Elo-style number.ELI5 §7 foot
11 · The famous Go/Dota bots are often summarised as "just vanilla self-play." Give two reasons that summary is wrong.
(1) The family used different recipes across generations — one drew random frozen opponents (fictitious play), one kept a 55% promotion gate, later ones dropped the gate. (2) Their wins leaned on ingredients outside opponent selection — a lookahead search that generates better-than-policy targets — plus transitive game structure Pokémon lacks.ELI5 §7
12 · Bonus: name one thing the survey says nothing about, that this project also needs.
Team search — which team to bring. The survey maps battle-policy training only; synergy measurement and the $f$ predictor strand live entirely outside it.Research report self-play-survey-vs-vault-gap.md
