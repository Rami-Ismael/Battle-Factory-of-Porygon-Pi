## Focused method notes

**S074 NGS — MAYBE, selective MED.** Read §3 and routing controlled comparisons in §5.1 of [fulltext](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.10433v2). Its pretrained generator is the same across search baselines, which supports isolating the search operator. Proposed adaptation: same checkpoint, starting population, evaluated-team/battle counts, compare ordinary sampling, one-parent completion, and two-parent typed restrictions. Mutations lift parent restrictions. It does not need optimal parent-to-child training pairs, unlike some learned-crossover methods. But the sequential factorization must be mapped to masked diffusion; retention and legality rules must be matched. Report finalist quality and random-draw quality separately. Parent restriction is not CEM fine-tuning.

**S064 SRT — DEFER despite appealing relevance.** The final [ICML paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/kim24o.html), §§4–5, includes hardware-design evidence and symmetric-versus-nonsymmetric replay comparisons. It is not molecule-only. However, ordinary slot-shuffled training is already implemented here, so recommending simple permutation augmentation would be redundant. A later distinct study could vary update-to-data schedule with a dedicated symmetry-replay term versus the existing shuffled elite-denoising loss, but first establish that these are mathematically/operationally different in this model. Equivalent team serializations are not new teams, and policy/decoder invariance cannot simply be assumed.

**S068 GenSCO — DEFER.** Final PDF inspected: solution-enhancement training maps suboptimal/disrupted graph solutions toward optimal targets; inference alternates disruptions with flow generation. That makes it more demanding than merely retaining good teams and re-masking slots. It is not evidence that the existing CEM loop should be replaced, nor that fewer denoising steps save battle queries.

**S042 preference optimization — DEFER.** Fulltext confirms a real DIMES training comparison (Appendix F.1). It is relevant if preference/reward optimization is activated, but current decision history keeps that family deferred. No reward-noise handling guarantee transfers.

**S055 RLD — DEFER.** Published graph results use exact/approximate gradients. Official implementation explicitly separates an unreported fully black-box REINFORCE option; that option must not be presented as published sample-efficiency evidence.
