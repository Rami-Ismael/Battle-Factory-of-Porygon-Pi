# DIMES citation screening: S001–S037

Screened 2026-09-08. Counts are the parent audit’s Semantic Scholar snapshot, not reconciled across duplicate records. Every item was checked against a primary author abstract on arXiv or venue page. Six plausible/important cases additionally checked in primary full text (method and relevant experiments); these are not claims of exhaustive replication. No active list edits.

Decisions: {'DEFER': 17, 'ALREADY': 1, 'SKIP': 16, 'MAYBE': 2, 'ADD': 1}

S033 is a duplicate/version record of S001, not another independent work.

## S001 — Evolution of Heuristics: Towards Efficient Automatic Algorithm Design Using Large Language Model

**DEFER** · 371 citations · evidence: abstract

Evolves LLM-written heuristic thoughts and code, rather than the candidate-team distribution. Revisit only for the automated-operator branch; it does not supply a new current CEM, legality or battle-evaluation experiment. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02051).

## S002 — DIFUSCO: Graph-based Diffusion Solvers for Combinatorial Optimization

**ALREADY** · 331 citations · evidence: abstract

Already in the neural CO queue and explained in this conversation. Graph bit denoising and task-specific decoding are background; no duplicate addition. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08224).

## S003 — Neural Combinatorial Optimization with Heavy Decoder: Toward Large Scale Generalization

**SKIP** · 233 citations · evidence: abstract

Light encoder/heavy autoregressive decoder targets generalization from small to large TSP/CVRP node counts. Teams remain six sets, and the masked diffusion architecture has a different bottleneck; no identified current transfer beyond existing partial regeneration. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.07985).

## S004 — DeepACO: Neural-enhanced Ant Systems for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 152 citations · evidence: abstract

Learns heuristic measures for an existing ant-colony solver. Requires a new team-construction/pheromone representation and objective-feedback training; GFACS is the more focused conditional reading if comparing multimodal proposal priors. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14032).

## S005 — From Distribution Learning in Training to Gradient Search in Testing for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 137 citations · evidence: abstract

Noise-and-reconstruct test-time gradient search is potentially relevant only with a compatible differentiable team objective or surrogate. Battles provide no such gradient; existing guidance readings cover the nearer prerequisite. Do not interpret TSP/MIS gains as noisy black-box evidence. [Primary source](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/9c93b3cd3bc60c0fe7b0c2d74a2da966-Abstract-Conference.html).

## S006 — Learning to Search Feasible and Infeasible Regions of Routing Problems with Flexible Neural k-Opt

**SKIP** · 123 citations · evidence: abstract

Flexible k-opt and infeasible-region exploration use routing-specific moves, feasibility features and shaped rewards. Illegal teams cannot supply battle fitness; this does not replace the current legality verifier or explain categorical team completion. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18264).

## S007 — BQ-NCO: Bisimulation Quotienting for Efficient Neural Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 115 citations · evidence: abstract

Bisimulation quotienting reduces constructive MDP state spaces using recursive problem symmetries. A formal team-construction MDP and proven equivalences would be prerequisites; this is beyond the current diffusion proposer and already planned team-order checks. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.03313).

## S008 — RL4CO: An Extensive Reinforcement Learning for Combinatorial Optimization Benchmark

**DEFER** · 111 citations · evidence: abstract

A benchmark/library for many CO environments and RL baselines, useful if building a constructive team-policy environment. It is engineering infrastructure, not a new method for the current diffusion/CEM experiment. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17100).

## S009 — Towards Omni-generalizable Neural Methods for Vehicle Routing Problems

**DEFER** · 105 citations · evidence: abstract

Meta-learns initialization for fast adaptation across routing sizes and distributions. Could matter after many opponent-specific optimization tasks exist; currently EAS and DIMES cover the adaptation question without another routing meta-learning paper. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19587).

## S010 — MVMoE: Multi-Task Vehicle Routing Solver with Mixture-of-Experts

**SKIP** · 94 citations · evidence: abstract

Mixture-of-experts generalizes among VRP variants. The project does not currently need one network covering many routing constraints; model-capacity scaling alone is insufficient reason to adopt a specialized MoE architecture. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.01029).

## S011 — Unsupervised Learning for Solving the Travelling Salesman Problem

**SKIP** · 88 citations · evidence: abstract

Unsupervised heatmaps optimize explicit tour-length and Hamiltonian-cycle surrogate losses before local search. There is no equivalent known differentiable battle objective; it adds a TSP-specific energy and decoder, not an applicable CEM update. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.10538).

## S012 — How Efficient is LLM-Generated Code? A Rigorous & High-Standard Benchmark

**SKIP** · 77 citations · evidence: abstract

ENAMEL measures efficiency of LLM-generated programs. Its code benchmark and reference implementations do not address generation of teams, noisy win-rate ranking, or training the team proposer. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06647).

## S013 — Winner Takes It All: Training Performant RL Populations for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 75 citations · evidence: fulltext

Poppy trains complementary policies for expected best-of-K return, updating the winner with a best-minus-runner-up signal (§3.2). This is distinct from improving average unranked team quality and has no strict diversity guarantee. Revisit for a portfolio objective and reward-based proposer training; compare one policy sampled K times against K specialized policies with equal battles and fresh finalist evaluation. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03475).

## S014 — Ant Colony Sampling with GFlowNets for Combinatorial Optimization

**MAYBE** · 69 citations · evidence: fulltext

Selective MED: §§5.1/5.5 and Appendix D.2 test whether a diverse learned prior helps the same downstream search, including active search beyond ACO. For teams, compare proposal distributions before/after an identical partial-regeneration search while controlling battles and initial quality; measure diversity and verified fitness. GFlowNet/pheromone implementation is unnecessary for this reading, and the paper does not establish noisy-battle gains. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07041).

## S015 — Fast T2T: Optimization Consistency Speeds Up Diffusion-Based Training-to-Testing Solving for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 60 citations · evidence: abstract

Consistency training accelerates diffusion and gradient-guided test-time search. Read only if denoising overhead becomes a measured bottleneck and suitable strong labels/surrogate exist; fewer denoising steps do not imply fewer battle queries or better diversity. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02941).

## S016 — Learning to Handle Complex Constraints for Vehicle Routing Problems

**DEFER** · 59 citations · evidence: abstract

Proactive infeasibility prevention learns masks to avoid dead ends when checking extendability is hard. Potential only if current categorical decoding repeatedly reaches uncompletable teams despite local legality masks. First measure that failure; VRP mask labels and reward shaping are not transferable as-is. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21066).

## S017 — Let the Flows Tell: Solving Graph Combinatorial Optimization Problems with GFlowNets

**DEFER** · 59 citations · evidence: abstract

Conditional GFlowNets supply diverse solution sampling and credit assignment, but require constructing a new team MDP and reward-based training. GFACS gives a more focused reading on priors plus downstream search; do not add both for the generic diversity analogy. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17010).

## S018 — Neural Combinatorial Optimization Algorithms for Solving Vehicle Routing Problems: A Comprehensive Survey with Perspectives

**SKIP** · 53 citations · evidence: abstract

Comprehensive routing NCO survey; the owner explicitly excludes surveys and no primary experimental contribution is needed for the current loop. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00415).

## S019 — INViT: A Generalizable Routing Problem Solver with Invariant Nested View Transformer

**SKIP** · 52 citations · evidence: abstract

Invariant nested views target geometric routing size/distribution generalization. Team fields do not have Euclidean routing views; no concrete current experiment warrants this architecture. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.02317).

## S020 — Select and Optimize: Learning to aolve large-scale TSP instances

**SKIP** · 51 citations · evidence: abstract

Selects and repairs TSP subproblems for large-instance inference. Partial regeneration is already proposed and NLNS is already peripheral; another route-segment selector lacks a distinct transferable experiment for six interacting sets. [Primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v206/cheng23a.html).

## S021 — ReasonFlux-PRM: Trajectory-Aware PRMs for Long Chain-of-Thought Reasoning in LLMs

**SKIP** · 48 citations · evidence: abstract

Process reward models supervise LLM reasoning trajectories. Team generation has no stepwise reasoning-trace target, and reward integration is deferred; selecting high-quality data in general is not enough relevance. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18896).

## S022 — GOAL: A Generalist Combinatorial Optimization Agent Learner

**DEFER** · 48 citations · evidence: abstract

Generalist graph solver with lightweight adapters for new CO problems. Requires a new graph representation and policy interface; only revisit if cross-problem pretraining becomes an explicit project direction. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15079).

## S023 — Rethinking Light Decoder-based Solvers for Vehicle Routing Problems

**SKIP** · 42 citations · evidence: abstract

Repairs capacity/generalization limits in light autoregressive routing decoders. Static node embeddings and larger VRP instances are not identified bottlenecks in the current masked team generator. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00753).

## S024 — Meta-SAGE: Scale Meta-Learning Scheduled Adaptation with Guided Exploration for Mitigating Scale Shift on Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 40 citations · evidence: abstract

Adapts routing embeddings to scale shifts, with nearby-location exploration bias. Team count is fixed and no corresponding geometric locality is established; EAS already supplies the relevant small-parameter adaptation reading. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02688).

## S025 — Cross-Problem Learning for Solving Vehicle Routing Problems

**DEFER** · 38 citations · evidence: abstract

Transfers a TSP backbone to related VRP variants using adapters. Relevant only if adapting an established team backbone across regulations/opponents becomes a measured issue; routing feature modules themselves do not transfer. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.11677).

## S026 — CO-Bench: Benchmarking Language Model Agents in Algorithm Search for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 37 citations · evidence: abstract

CO-Bench evaluates LLM agents that search for algorithms. Use if resuming automated heuristic design, not as an addition for the present candidate-generator experiments. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.04310).

## S027 — Revisiting Sampling for Combinatorial Optimization

**DEFER** · 35 citations · evidence: fulltext

Useful corrective background: iSCO shows training-free sampling can be competitive. But §§3.2.1–3.2.2 depend on cheap local energy differences/gradients and MH correction, unavailable directly from noisy battles. Current mutation/local-search controls already test the core baseline lesson; do not propose iSCO as a drop-in black-box team solver. [Primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/sun23c.html).

## S028 — Diffusion Models as Network Optimizers: Explorations and Analysis

**SKIP** · 34 citations · evidence: abstract

Diffusion network optimization learns conditional high-quality solution distributions and samples repeatedly. That premise is already understood; no verified new CEM/noisy-oracle mechanism justifies another domain application paper. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00453).

## S029 — Self-Improved Learning for Scalable Neural Combinatorial Optimization

**ADD** · 34 citations · evidence: fulltext

MED, read §4 and Figure 3: local reconstruction creates better pseudo-labels, then supervised learning improves the next reconstruction round. Concrete comparison: global top-fraction CEM versus training on independently validated improvements retained per starting team, at matched battle/training counts; also compare frozen reconstruction. TSP can evaluate/merge subpaths locally; teams must be evaluated as complete interacting objects, and noisy improvements require fresh validation. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19561).

## S030 — GDSG: Graph Diffusion-Based Solution Generator for Optimization Problems in MEC Networks

**SKIP** · 31 citations · evidence: fulltext

Training from suboptimal labels matches a live axis, but §III-B assumes mean suboptimal parameterization equals the optimum and independent bits for its hitting example. Experiments compare datasets differing in size/scale (e.g., 80k suboptimal versus 2k optimal), so they do not resolve the planned matched-data-quality ablation. Existing experiment plan is stronger for this question; no new priority. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08296).

## S031 — DiffSG: A Generative Solver for Network Optimization with Diffusion Model

**SKIP** · 31 citations · evidence: abstract

Diffusion proposal sampling for network optimization is a domain demonstration. It supplies neither the current evaluate-select-retrain loop nor treatment of battle noise, and the generative-versus-discriminative argument is already covered. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06701).

## S032 — Position: Rethinking Post-Hoc Search-Based Neural Approaches for Solving Large-Scale Traveling Salesman Problems

Selective MED for §§3–5: replace learned TSP heatmaps with a simple baseline while preserving the downstream search to test attribution. Adapt as proposer-source × identical ranking/repair/search ablation, measuring before/after search with fresh battles. Directly challenges over-crediting DIFUSCO/DIMES components, but overlaps the already-listed Unify ML4TSP evaluation reading; add only if this narrower audit is useful. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.03503).
