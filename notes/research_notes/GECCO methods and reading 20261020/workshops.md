# GECCO workshops and track fit

Checked October 20, 2026. These rankings assess fit to the current project: discrete legal team proposals, expensive noisy battles, a possible learned scorer or generator, and higher independently evaluated battle win rate as the goal. They do not rank venue prestige or predict acceptance. Workshop relevance and an individual paper's reading grade are separate decisions.

## What was actually listed in 2026

The official list contains 17 workshops. It includes SAEOpt, AABOH, ECADA, BENCH, decomposition, LLMfwEC, and open-source software. It does not list Landscape-Aware Heuristic Search or Evolutionary Computation in Games. Landscape-Aware Heuristic Search appears in the **2025** companion proceedings. The **2026** programme has a landscape-analysis tutorial instead. Do not carry an older workshop into the 2026 or 2027 programme by assumption. [2026 workshops](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Workshops), [2026 tutorials](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorials), [2025 companion proceedings](https://www.sigevo.org/gecco-2025/toc-companion.html).

## Ranked main tracks

These are main-conference research tracks, distinct from workshops and tutorials. A finished paper should follow its actual contribution.

| Rank | Track | Contribution that would fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [ECOM: Evolutionary Combinatorial Optimization and Metaheuristics](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=52) | Better search over legal, categorical teams. Its scope explicitly covers neighbourhoods, representations, constraints, landscape analysis, Walsh decomposition, and algorithm behaviour used to improve optimisation. |
| 2 | [L4EC: Learning for Evolutionary Computation](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=2671) | A learned generator, scorer, operator controller, or landscape model that improves evolutionary search. It explicitly includes surrogate-assisted expensive evaluation, multi-fidelity approaches, and knowledge extracted from trajectories or populations. |
| 3 | [GECH: General Evolutionary Computation and Hybrids](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=57) | A justified combination of global evolution with local search, constructive heuristics, exact methods, MCTS, multiple fidelities or resolutions, or landscape analysis **inside the search**. This page is the source of the user's quoted hybrid-method list. |
| 4 | [BBSR: Benchmarking, Benchmarks, Software, and Reproducibility](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=2672) | A reusable benchmark, evaluation protocol, diagnostic method, software contribution, or rigorous replication. Landscape analysis and statistical visualisation are explicitly included. |
| 5 | [GA: Genetic Algorithms](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=56) | A contribution centred on GA representation, selection, recombination, mutation, uncertainty, or population diversity. It also includes landscape analysis, surrogates, and model-based GAs. |
| 6 | [RWA: Real World Applications](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=60) | A genuinely new application formulation. Games are in scope, but the call excludes work that merely uses an existing public real-world benchmark. An algorithmic improvement on an established benchmark belongs in the matching methods track. |

If the main novelty is the learned search component, move L4EC to first. If the main novelty is the hybrid's coordination mechanism, move GECH to first. For a diagnostics-only contribution, BBSR moves above the methods tracks. ECOM expects an anonymised code/data repository link in the initial submission. [ECOM scope](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=52).

The lower-priority tracks are conditional on a changed question: GP for evolving programmes or explicit heuristics, EML or NE for evolving/training the battle model or policy, EMO for a genuinely multiobjective research goal, SI for a swarm-specific advance, THEORY for formal results, and CS for a complex-systems contribution. L4EC uses learning to improve evolutionary search; EML uses evolutionary computation to solve machine-learning problems. The current project emphasis points toward L4EC. [Track inventory](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tracks), [L4EC distinction](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Track?itemId=2671).

## Tutorials to study separately

1. [Landscape Analysis of Optimization Problems and Algorithms](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorial?itemId=8226), Gabriela Ochoa and Katherine Malan. The direct match to the user's new interest. Treat it as learning material rather than an accepted research-paper result.
2. [Evolutionary Computation meets Machine Learning for Combinatorial Optimisation](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorial?itemId=8207), Yi Mei and Günther Raidl. Useful for deciding whether to learn candidates, heuristics, or decision policies.
3. [Model-Based Evolutionary Algorithms](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorial?itemId=8215), Dirk Thierens and Peter Bosman. Relevant background for learning distributions and dependencies.
4. [Advanced Use of Automatic Algorithm Configuration](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorial?itemId=8228), Jeroen Rook and Manuel López-Ibáñez. Relevant if systematically configuring the search across tasks becomes the research contribution.

These are learning priorities for the requested topics, not grades under the project's categorical-paper policy. Titles, speakers, and category are confirmed by the official tutorial list. This review did not inspect their recordings. [2026 tutorial list](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Tutorials).

## Verified paper leads and why they do not automatically enter the active queue

The project reading-list policy supplied by the integrator grades continuous-only or toy evaluations LOW. Apply that policy even when a paper offers an interesting diagnostic idea. Do not promote these to MED merely because their workshop is highly relevant.

| Paper | Evidence checked | Project decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Analyzing Flexible Search Distributions in Black-Box Optimization with Normalizing Flow-based Estimation of Distribution Algorithms](https://doi.org/10.1145/3795101.3814685), Karami and Gagné, AABOH 2026 | Official workshop acceptance and the full abstract on the authors' institutional publication page. NF-EDA learns a flexible distribution, with a Gaussian EDA ablation using matched update rules. Tests are selected continuous COCO BBOB functions. | **LOW, explanatory lead.** The useful experimental idea is to isolate distribution expressiveness from the surrounding update procedure. There is no categorical-team or noisy-battle validation. [Institutional abstract](https://mila.quebec/en/research/publications?page=8&q=Generative+Models). |
| [Beyond Final Fitness: Lagged Behavioural Diagnostics for Analysing Optimisation Heuristics](https://doi.org/10.1145/3795101.3814672), Salgotra, AABOH 2026 | Official workshop acceptance and institutional abstract. FGCI measures conversion of present activity into future gain; ERI measures rebound after stagnation. The abstract explicitly says these are diagnostics rather than final-quality ranking metrics. Tests are continuous optimisation. | **LOW, explanatory lead.** Useful for deciding what to log and how to distinguish activity from progress. Full formula/implementation details were not verified here. Do not replace battle win rate with either index. [Institutional abstract](https://badap.agh.edu.pl/publikacja/169992). |
| [From 'How Good?' to 'Is it Better?': Classification-Based Surrogate Models for JADE](https://doi.org/10.1145/3712255.3734539), Krawczyk and Arabas, SAEOpt 2025 | Official proceedings abstract and programme establish three relative-quality classifiers tested with JADE on CEC 2013. | **LOW, scorer-design background.** Predicting whether one candidate is better is an interesting alternative to exact-score regression. Its continuous-benchmark evidence does not validate noisy comparisons between teams. [Official abstract and workshop session](https://www.sigevo.org/gecco-2025/toc-companion.html). |
| [Mutation Without Variation: Convergence Dynamics in LLM-Driven Program Evolution](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05408v1), Gurkan, Stonedahl, and Wilensky, LLMfwEC 2026 | Author full text and official workshop acceptance. It studies neutral LLM mutation chains without selection, using a constrained programme language for gridworld agents. | **LOW, conditional operator diagnosis.** The design separates mutation bias from selection pressure. It studies programme structures rather than VGC candidates or win rate; it does not establish how diffusion team sampling behaves. |

The first, second, and fourth papers are confirmed on the [2026 accepted-workshop list](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/Accepted%2BWorkshop%2BPapers). The third appears under SAEOpt in the [2025 programme](https://gecco-2025.sigevo.org/display1875). They have different verification depth: institutional abstract for NF-EDA and Beyond Final Fitness, proceedings abstract for JADE, and author full text for Mutation Without Variation.

Two potentially relevant **title-only holds** from the 2026 list are *Optimizing Unrelated Parallel Machine Scheduling with Non-Resumable Maintenance via Surrogate Models and Exact Dynamic Programming*, SAEOpt, and *LLM-Driven Configuration of Genetic Algorithms for Constraint-Rich Optimization Problems*, ECADA. Their acceptance is verified, but this review did not obtain a primary abstract or full text sufficient to grade their actual methods and experiments. The latter is also confirmed on the [authors' university publication list](https://web.uri.edu/comparch/publications/) and [ECADA programme](https://bonsai.auburn.edu/ecada/GECCO2026/index.html). Keep them out of the active queue until screened beyond the title.

## Limits and publication timing

This screen establishes the actual 2026 workshop inventory, selected scopes, and a small set of paper leads. It does not claim an exhaustive full-text reading of all 84 accepted workshop contributions. Do not infer the 2027 workshop roster or submission dates from these historical pages. The existing GECCO report records the partly updated 2027 call and its stale dates. Any eventual submission choice needs the corrected call and the final research contribution.
