# Refutation pass: which samplers the seven masked models use (2026-11-18)

This pass checks `masked-text-diffusion-decoding-by-model-2026-11-18.md` at commit `9a13cdd7` against the primary sources. Line numbers below are the note's own lines. A **sampler** is the loop that picks which masked positions to fill at each step. "Holds" means the source says what the note says. "Overstated" means the source says something narrower. "Wrong" means the source says something else. "Unverifiable" means this pass could not open or locate the evidence.

No models were run. Code was read at the revisions the note pins. Papers were read as arXiv HTML or the proceedings PDF. Numbers for the Dream and LLaDA cache papers (rows 47–53) were checked by confirming every quoted value appears in the paper text and by reading the table captions, not by re-reading every cell against its column.
