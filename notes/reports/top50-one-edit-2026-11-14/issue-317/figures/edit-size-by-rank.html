How far one edit moves a team, against how much the team wins

Each dot is one starting team: 49 top-50 meta teams, 24 tournament-ready teams, 16 search-found teams. Rank correlation -0.17; negative means teams that win more change more.

A dot's height is the typical move of that team's 18 edits, battle noise included; the dashed line is what replaying an unchanged team does. Each score is 384 shuffled-slot battles against all 50 meta files with the behaviour-cloning policy on both sides. Rank uses the mean of a team's three replays, not the first score its edits are measured against.
