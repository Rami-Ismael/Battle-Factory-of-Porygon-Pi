Team diversity: builds, Novelty, nearest training team, archetypes

At 30 uses of one species, the default model writes about 20.6 different builds where its training teams show 25.7, a gap more than seven times the widest difference between two training samples. Its teams sit 36 fields from the closest training team against 24 for a training team, none is a copy, and 59% reuse a training line-up exactly against 71%. It carries Trick Room and sun more often than its training teams.

Builds per species at equal uses

The three default-model bars sit near 20.6 builds and the three matched training bars near 25.7, while two training samples differ by at most 0.68 builds.

Novelty: species swaps to the closest training line-up

The default model reuses a training line-up exactly for 59% of its teams against 71% for matched training teams, and moves one or two species away more often; three or more swaps stays rare at 0.9% to 3.7%.

Nearest training distance

No team in any row copies a training team at all 84 fields; the default model's teams sit a median 34 fields from the closest one against 24 for training and validation teams.

Archetypes, setters only

Tailwind is the commonest archetype everywhere, on 42% to 50% of teams; the default model puts Trick Room on 28% to 37% of its teams and sun on 32% to 35%, against 25% and 29% for matched training teams.

Which listed setters exist in the Regulation M-B vocabulary

Every listed move exists in the vocabulary except Hail, which Generation 9 replaced with Snowscape. Of the listed abilities, Drought, Drizzle, Sand Stream, Sand Spit, Snow Warning and Electric Surge exist; Orichalcum Pulse, Desolate Land, Primordial Sea, Hadron Engine, Grassy Surge, Seed Sower, Misty Surge and Psychic Surge do not, so grassy, misty and psychic terrain can only come from the move. All five listed mega stones exist. A set stores its base ability: of 197 Charizard holding Charizardite Y in the first default-model sample, 1 stores Drought, and in the first matched training sample 4 of 228 do. Tyranitar and Abomasnow already have their setting ability as a base ability, and Froslass stores Snow Warning on 30 of 67 holders in the model sample against 3 of 53 in training. Without the stones, the sun archetype would miss nearly every Charizard Y team. Meganiumite is left out: Mega Sol sets no weather on the field.

Training teams drawn over all win rates keep about 900 line-ups but drop to 3,427 to 3,470 builds and 23.9 builds per species, and their builds and whole-team Vendi Scores fall to or below the default model's. The default model still writes half the line-ups and fewer builds per species than either training row.

Vendi Score between its anchors

At every level the training teams sit between one team repeated and random legal teams, so the score orders the anchors; the default model sits below the matched training teams on builds and the whole team, and level with or above the training teams drawn over all win rates.

What "diverse" means at each level

Line-up · 6 fields

The six species, unordered. Diverse means many different line-ups, no line-up taking a large share, and many different species pairs, since pairs of teammates carry a team.

Builds · 48 fields

Species, ability, item, Stat Alignment and four moves per Pokémon. Diverse means the same species appears with different builds, so two teams on one line-up can still differ.

Stat Points · 36 fields

Six numbers per Pokémon, 66 points in all. Diverse means different allocations for the same species. Random placement scores highest, so compare against the training teams instead of maximising.

Whole team · 84 fields

All of the above. Diverse means a high effective number of different teams, where a near-copy counts as partly the same team.

Rules fixed before the run

  • The default model differs from its training teams on a measure when all three seed differences share a sign and each lies outside the noise floor.
  • A measure is usable when the training value falls between the two anchors.
  • A row drawn from the training teams leaves each team out of its own reference once, for Novelty and for the nearest training distance. The exact-match novelty counts from 11-09 do not.
  • Every 11-09 value reproduces exactly: the added rows draw from their own random streams.

Settings carried over without a decision of their own

  • The model's teams come from the 11-03 ask grid as it ran: guidance 2, temperature 1, rule masks on, species first, Stat Alignment repaired to match the moves.
  • Asks 18/24 to 23/24 are left out of the matched rows, since the training data holds 610 teams or fewer there.
  • I picked 30 uses for builds per species, Sand Spit and the five mega stones as setters, and the bins of the nearest training distance. None was tuned on the result.

Caveats

  • The Vendi Score ignores duplicates by design. Read it beside distinct teams out of n.
  • More Stat Points diversity is not better. Random placement wins that contest, and the 11-04 swap test found the model's Stat Points ignore their Pokémon.
  • Archetypes count setters on the team, not whether a battle ever set the weather.
  • One model, one checkpoint, guidance 2 only. The guidance sweep reruns this script.
