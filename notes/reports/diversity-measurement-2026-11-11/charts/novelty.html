The default model reuses a training line-up exactly for 59% of its teams, against 71% for win-rate-matched training

It moves one or two species away more often; three or more swaps stays rare at 0.9% to 3.7%.

Swaps are six minus the species a team shares with the closest of the 170,785 training line-ups. Rows drawn from the training teams leave each team out of its own reference once, so a training row at zero swaps means another training team has the same line-up.
