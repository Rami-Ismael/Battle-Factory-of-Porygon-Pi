**The distinction to understand is measured score versus requested score.** Training pairs contain candidates and their measured objective values. At generation time, the method proposes conditioning targets relative to the best observed value, chooses a target, samples candidates, queries the true objective, and adds the new observations. Requesting `y` does not guarantee that a generated candidate achieves `y`. The optimization mechanism includes target selection and repeated feedback from evaluations, not merely accidental overshooting. [Section 4.1, Figure 1, and Algorithm 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.00610v3#S4.SS1)

UaE chooses the target score `y` using `α(y,D) = y − Δ_epistemic(y,D)`. It is not team-level UCB. Understanding what this uncertainty measures matters before adapting it to battle outcomes. [Section 5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.00610v3#S5.SS3)
