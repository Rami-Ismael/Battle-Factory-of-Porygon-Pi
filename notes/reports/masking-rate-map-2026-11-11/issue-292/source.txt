Departures: where does our 84-field discrete diffusion code depart from MD4 and LLaDA?

## Question

List every place the 84-field model's training (`code/src/diffusion84.py`, `code/src/lossdown.py`) and sampling depart from MD4 (Shi et al. 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04329) for training and masking rates, and from LLaDA (Nie et al. 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09992) for decoding. For each: what our code does (file and function), what the paper does (section, equation or algorithm, quoted), and whether it is our own choice with no published source. Candidates already known: the per-field masking rate, the t given while decoding, the fixed species-first order, the legal Pokémon construction filter's −10,000 in the loss and while sampling, slot shuffling, the ÷84 per-field average, t spread evenly within each team's validation draws, ask dropout, the time input. Read the papers' released code where the paper is silent (MD4: google-deepmind/md4; LLaDA: ML-GSAI/LLaDA).

Parent map: [Masking-rate map: per-field versus linear masking rate on the 84-field model, and where our code departs from MD4 and LLaDA](https://github.com/Rami-Ismael/Battle-Factory-of-Porygon-Pi/issues/291)


**Resolved 2026-11-11.** Findings: branch `research/diffusion84-departures`, file `code/docs/research/diffusion84-departures-from-md4-llada.md` (no PR; never merged).

Of 14 parts of the 84-field model, 3 follow the papers: t spread evenly across the batch (MD4 Algorithm 1), the time input (MD4 App. I.2) and guidance mixing (LLaDA Eq. 16). The rest are ours, with no published source: the per-field masking rate's fixed exponents (MD4 §6 learns them per token value), the ÷84 average, slot shuffling, the legal Pokémon construction filter in the loss and while sampling, the fixed species-first order, the sampler's t = 1 − filled/84, temperature, Stat Point tokens, the 0.15 ask dropout, and the validation draws' 4 samples with a fixed seed. Still unchecked: MD4 Algorithm 2's time grid against ours.

**Correction, 2026-11-11.** The division of the loss by 84 is not ours. LLaDA Algorithm 1 computes the loss as −1/(t × L) times the sum over hidden tokens, so dividing by the 84 fields follows it. MD4 Algorithm 1 sums without dividing. Found while checking citations for the pre-registration against each paper's LaTeX source: https://github.com/Rami-Ismael/Battle-Factory-of-Porygon-Pi/blob/t3/preregister-masking-rate-rows/code/docs/masking-rate-rows.md
