## The failed probe

The table shows how far the default model's drawing distribution moves when a pass is told another t, on the same partly filled teams. The measure is mean total variation, where 0 means the same distribution and 1 means no overlap. The bar set before the run was 0.01 on at least one field type. The baseline is today's sampler, which tells the model t = 1 − filled/84.

| Rule the pass is told | Species | Ability and item | Moves | Stat Alignment | Stat Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corrected t | 0.0007 | 0.0010 | 0.0025 | 0.0040 | 0.0030 |
| Always t = 1 | 0.0011 | 0.0019 | 0.0067 | 0.0149 | 0.0275 |
| Always t = 1/84 | 0.0369 | 0.0109 | 0.0123 | 0.0118 | 0.0066 |

The corrected t never reaches the bar. With the same sampling seed it changes 1% to 2% of fields and 1 line-up in 768 teams. This agrees with both papers: MD4 §3 says the network can be made independent of t, and LLaDA Appendix A.1 gives its network no t. It also agrees with the 2026-10-07 run that removed the time input and lost nothing (`code/docs/loss-down.md`, run `N1`). So the sampler's t departs from the papers and has no measurable effect on this model.

## What else the pre-registration found

| Finding | Number | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today's early-stopping threshold would cut the new training short | Replayed on the control's own curve, a threshold of 0.001 stops at epoch 47. The control ran to 80 | The new training must pass `early_stopping_threshold=0.0`, or training length mixes with the masking rate |
| Today's code reproduces the control's training | Epoch 1 lands on training loss 1.7917 against the logged 1.7918, validation 1.47690 against 1.47691 | The new training differs from the control in the masking rate alone |
| The error has a ceiling near asks 13/24 to 17/24 | 91% of the control's 0.0053 comes from those five asks. Asks 1/24 to 12/24 alone score 0.0007 | A row can only gain by lifting got above about 0.53 |
| Sampling seeds share an effect | Seed 1 reads the highest error on 15 of 20 stored 3-seed grids | Rows are compared seed by seed. Paired, the budget detects a gap of about 0.001 |
| One training seed limits the linear rows | Two trainings of one recipe have differed by up to 0.0019 in error and 0.026 in pooled win rate | The linear rows are exploratory, and a gap under 0.002 is reported as inside training luck |
| The division by 84 is published | LLaDA Algorithm 1 divides the loss by t × L | The departures ticket listed it as ours. That entry is wrong |
| A loss is only comparable under one masking rate | The default model scores 1.209 under its own per-field rate and 1.404 under the linear rate | The linear model's validation loss is read against 1.404 |

Every citation in the document was checked against each paper's LaTeX source from arXiv. Cost after this ticket: about 5.4 hours of training and 710,000 battles, about 2.5 hours, all on the laptop.
