Rerun the one-edit study per edit kind with shuffled slots against the full meta

## What this run answers

How much does one legal edit to a team move its win rate, for each of six kinds of edit, when every battle shuffles slot order and the opponents are the full meta?

The smoothness post wants to say an edit can be a move, an item or a whole Pokemon. The only numbers for items and whole Pokemon come from the 2026-09-28 study, which faced 36 of the 50 meta files and battled in listed slot order. Listed-order battles never count (owner, 2026-11-07). This run replaces those numbers.

## What runs

| | Rerun | 2026-09-28 study |
|---|---|---|
| Opponents | all 50 meta files, 49 distinct teams (MB493 and MB494 are the same team, weight 2) | 36 of the 50 |
| Slot order | shuffled every battle, both sides | listed |
| Starting teams | 24 meta teams, 16 search-found teams | same |
| Edit kinds | one move, ability, held item, stat alignment, Stat Points, whole Pokemon; 3 edits each | same kinds, edits drawn again by the same seeded code |
| Battles per score | 384, behaviour-cloning policy on both sides | 384 |
| Tests | T1 beyond battle noise, T2 rougher than Pest Control, cutoffs unchanged | same |

**Verdict, in the pre-registered wording:** win rate is rugged under a single legal edit, beyond battle noise and beyond Pest Control, for one edit type: replacing a whole Pokemon with a real set of a new species. That holds from both the 24 meta teams (5.90 points, neighbour correlation 0.787 [0.576, 0.881]) and the 16 search-found teams (5.85 points, 0.791 [0.580, 0.906]). September reached the same verdict for the same edit type.

**Run against plan:** 880 scores, each 384 battles, 337,920 battles in all. 50 opponent files, 49 distinct teams. Every battle shuffled slots. 3 edits per kind per team, 3 replicates per team. All as planned. The sphere sanity check passes (0.994).

**What this cannot show**
- That a move edit is small. It misses T1 by 0.16 and 0.08 points on a cutoff, and the analysis saves no interval for that size.
- Direction. The statistic squares the change, so a large edit can be a harmful one.
- Anything about good teams. The search-found teams average 0.445 against the meta under shuffled slots, below the meta teams' 0.507, so they are not a high-scoring group.
- A corrected family of tests. Twelve cells are tested and none is corrected for multiple testing. Intervals resample starting teams only.
- Other opponents or another battle policy. One pool, one behaviour-cloning policy.
