Prototype · label-cap experiment, 2026-10-31

Removing the strong teams did not widen the model

We retrained the discrete diffusion model from scratch without a single team labelled above 0.5, to see whether the narrow strong slice of the data causes the collapse.

Win rate: the fraction of 49 battles a team wins, one against each top-50 meta team, with the VGC-Bench behaviour-cloning policy on both sides. Ask: the win rate the model is told to aim for. Line-up: a team's six species, slot order ignored. Capped model: trained with no team above 0.5. Control model: trained on the same number of random labelled teams.
