VGC schemes: weather, speed control, and combinations

Checked 11 October 2026: TrickRoom’s current M-C catalog lists Rain, Sun, Sand, Snow, Trick Room, Tailwind and Balance. This visualization applies that vocabulary to the recorded M-B checkpoint; it does not claim current M-C legality or usage rates for these samples.

VGC Guide: Speed Control, by Wolfe Glick explains Tailwind and Trick Room. His Cores and Modes explains why teams can combine plans. Here, a tag means the required move or weather-setting ability is present, not that the generated team uses it well. Trick Room can also be used to counter an opponent’s Trick Room.

Weather definitions: Rain, Sun, Sand, Snow. We check actual abilities, manual weather moves, and compatible weather Mega forms/stones in the pinned M-B dex. Species names alone do not establish weather access.

VGC Guide: Archetypes, by Aaron Traylor relates archetypes to shared game plans and notes that some teams do not fit one. We leave unmatched samples Unclassified. We do not infer Balance, hyper offense or hard Trick Room from missing weather or one move; those need stronger evidence about the full team’s function.
