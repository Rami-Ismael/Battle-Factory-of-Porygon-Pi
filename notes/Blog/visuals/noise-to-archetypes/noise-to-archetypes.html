Archetypes

    An archetype is the first rule that fires on the revealed fields, in the repository's priority order (diffusion.RULES): Trick Room, rain, sun, sand, snow, Tailwind. Grey dots have no rule yet. Hover a dot to see its partly masked team.

    How a partly masked team gets a position

    pos(xt) = A(xt) + ·Q φ(xt) + ·√mt · ε
    A(xt)
    The anchor of the archetype the revealed tokens already imply: six rule archetypes on a unit ring, and none (or not decided yet) at the centre. A dot jumps toward an anchor at the step its Drizzle, Trick Room or Tailwind is sampled.
    Q φ(xt)
    The top two principal components of the real corpus's field features (species, ability, item, moves, nature), scaled to unit variance. They place similar teams near each other inside a cluster. A masked field enters as its corpus-average one-hot, so it adds zero.
    mt
    The fraction of the 48 fields still masked. It starts at 1 and ends at 0.
    ε
    A fixed standard Gaussian per team. The √mt term makes it a pure Gaussian cloud at step 0 and exact at step 48.

    What is real here, and what is drawn

    Real: the teams, the order they unmask in, and the archetype each one lands in. Sampling is unconditional at decoding temperature 1.0, with the repository's constrained decoder and its dependency order: six species, then abilities and items, then moves, then natures. Seed 7. No Showdown validation filter was applied.

    Drawn: the geometry. Masked diffusion masks fields; it doesn't add Gaussian noise. So ε stands in for the fields that are still masked, and the anchor ring is a chosen layout, not a learned embedding. I tried linear maps from all 48 fields first (discriminant analysis and ridge regression onto the anchors). The archetypes overlapped, because only a handful of tokens define them and the other fields drown those tokens out.
