Noise to Team Archetypes

A diffusion model starts from static and removes it a little at a time. Here the thing it builds is a Pokémon team, and the figure below builds one on screen.

A team here is six Pokémon, and each one has a species, an ability, an item, four moves and a nature. That makes 48 fields. The generator decides them in order, one field per step, starting from nothing. Each decision narrows what the rest of the team can be.

Four archetypes sort teams by how they win: Trick Room, Weather, Tailwind and Other. A future is one of the 24 ways the model could still finish MB607, a real Reg M-B team and the running example, at a given step. The left panel shows 300 teams the model generated from nothing, each at the same step.
