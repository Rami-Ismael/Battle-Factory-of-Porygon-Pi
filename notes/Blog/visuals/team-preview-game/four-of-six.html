Four of six

Before every VGC battle, both players see each other's six Pokémon and secretly choose four to bring and two to lead. That choice is a small game of its own. Step through it with two real meta teams.

15 × 6 = 90 plans

Picking leads is a split into two groups, not an ordering: two start, two wait in the back, two stay home. 6! ÷ (2!·2!·2!) = 90.

Showdown's ordered input: 6 · 5 · 4 · 3 = 360
360 ÷ 4 = 90

Swapping your two leads between left and right, or your two back Pokémon, gives the same plan. Each plan appears 4 times among the 360 orderings. (Illusion on Zoroark-Hisui is the one exception.)

You pick a row, they pick a column, at the same moment. 90 × 90 = 8,100 pairings; each cell is a chance to win. Cells are left blank: this matrix has not been measured.
