    Source: VGC-Bench (Angliss, Cui, Hu, Rahman and Stone, AAMAS 2026), Appendix A. The paper sets up the stat-spread count and gives the answer, 246,774,528, but not the working. The stars-and-bars subtraction shown here reproduces that number exactly.

    It is an upper bound for Scarlet/Violet: it assumes every species has about 100 moves and lets two Pokémon hold the same item. It is not the Champions count. For scale: chess positions ≈ 4.8 × 1044 (Tromp), chess games ≈ 10120 (Shannon, 1950), Go positions ≈ 2.1 × 10170 (Tromp, 2016).
