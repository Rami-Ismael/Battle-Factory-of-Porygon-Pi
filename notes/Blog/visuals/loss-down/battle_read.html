The new model’s teams win more in every one of the ten cells: by 0.07 with no conditioning and by up to 0.12 when asked for a win rate. Eight of the ten differences clear two standard errors. Its best cell, asked for 0.5 at guidance 2, reaches 0.402 ± 0.013, against the old model’s best of 0.322 (asked for 0.5 at guidance 4). That is the strongest any diffusion model in this project has written, and it is still well below a real top-50 team at 0.531.

Two cautions. The gap closes at guidance 4, where the old model needed the extra push and the new one starts to overshoot. And the strongest cell is the least varied: 24 different line-ups of six Pokémon among its 64 teams, against 43 for the old model in the same cell. Legality went the other way: 97–100% of the new model’s samples pass Showdown’s validator, against 74–98% for the old one, and none of the 640 teams copies a known team.
