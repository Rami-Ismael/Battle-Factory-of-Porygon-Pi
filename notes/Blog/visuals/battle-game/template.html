Turn one

After team preview, the battle itself is a game: two players, one winner, moves chosen at the same time, dice everywhere, and much of the board hidden. Step through each property on the first turn of a real matchup.

Whatever one side's chance of winning, the other side has the rest. The split moves as the battle goes; the total never does. (The moving numbers are an illustration.)

Choosing…

Each of the four Pokémon on the field gets an order. Nothing happens until both players have locked in, and neither sees the other's orders first.

A move aimed at one Pokémon can target either opponent or your own partner, so it counts three times. Your two Pokémon can't both switch into the same back Pokémon, hence the −2.
