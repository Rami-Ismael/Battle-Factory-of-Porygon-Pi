Which Pokémon each version keeps reaching for

Seven versions of the discrete diffusion model, each asked for 576 teams at win rates 12/24, 0.531 and 13/24. Every shelf stacks the Pokémon a version used.

How to read a shelf. Each column is one Pokémon, in the same order on every shelf. One sprite = 10% of that version's teams. A tall column means the version puts that Pokémon on most of its teams.

Five versions, one core. The current model and the four retrains that change only the labels or the label mix all build from the same six Pokémon, each on 65–91% of their teams.

The species quotas break the core. They cap how many training teams may share a species, and their shelves flatten: more Pokémon used, none on more than three teams in four. The cost is win rate.
