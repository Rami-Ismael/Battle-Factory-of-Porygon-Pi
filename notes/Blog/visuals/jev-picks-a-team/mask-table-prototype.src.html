How many puzzles in a grid?

Five starting teams. Six kinds of blank: items, abilities, Stat Alignments, Stat Points, moves, whole Pokémon. One to six blanks of each, but a team has 24 move slots. Guess first.

Masks per grid

Each grid starts from five teams. A mask hides some fields of one team; a model fills them back in, and the filled team is battled. Pick a starting team, a kind of blank and how many blanks, and you see the real mask from the meta grid and the questions Jev would be asked.
