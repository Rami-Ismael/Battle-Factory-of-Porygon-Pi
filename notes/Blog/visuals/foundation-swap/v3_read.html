Version 3 is much worse as a plain sampler and a little better with everything stacked. Plain, its teams win 0.099, against 0.257 for the new model. Half of those plain teams (33 of 64) hold a species the old vocabulary could not write, and those teams win 0.010, almost never; the other half win 0.194. The new species entered training through 6,000 synthetic teams that carry no win rates, so the model learned that Vivillon-Savanna and Gallade-Mega exist, but not how to use them.

Every method that steers toward winning drops the new species again: asking for 0.5, the predictor’s pick and the full stack put them on none of their 64 teams, and land at or a little below the new model. Fully stacked, version 3 reaches 0.556 ± 0.012, the highest of any model here and above a real top-50 team, with 38 of 64 teams over that line. But 38 of its 64 teams are also a single line-up (Basculegion, Chandelure, Floette-Eternal, Garchomp, Kingambit and Whimsicott, at 0.585), and its lead over the new model, +0.029, spans only 1.7 standard errors.

So the fixes did their job, which was to let the model read and write every legal team, and they did not make it a better team writer on their own. The newly reachable species need battle-labelled teams before they can help.
