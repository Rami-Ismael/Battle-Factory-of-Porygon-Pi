Teaching example

The paper’s central equation, translated
θt+1 = arg maxθ ∑i wi log qθ(xi)Choose generator settings that make the weighted batch more likely. This is weighted maximum likelihood; q is shorthand for the paper’s p(x | θ).
xi
One complete candidate design in the batch.
qθ(x)
The generator’s probability of producing that design. θ means its adjustable settings.
wi
The oracle’s probability that candidate i satisfies the current goal.
log qθ(xi)
A training score that increases when the generator assigns more probability to candidate i.
∑ and arg max
Add the weighted training scores; choose settings with the largest total.

The three-candidate example has a closed-form update: divide each candidate’s total weight by the sum of all weights. A neural generator instead learns shared patterns through its training procedure.

DbAS, §2, equation 13. The numerical examples on this page are invented for teaching, not reproduced experiments.
