Archaludon losing its instant Electro Shot needs both other Pokémon: Pelipper (the rain it had) and Charizard (the sun that replaced it). Pelipper + Archaludon alone is good. Charizard + Archaludon alone changes nothing, since Electro Shot charges without rain either way. So the loss comes from the three together: a third-order effect.
