A few ways to write

Use Markdown as you type: # for a heading, - for a list, and > for a quote, followed by a space.

⌘ / Ctrl + B for bold, I for italic, Z to undo, and S to save. Select text and choose “Highlight & note” to begin an editing pass.

Use the arrows in the margin to move through open suggestions. Resolve a note when you’re done, or accept its suggested wording.
