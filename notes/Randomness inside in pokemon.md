# Writing

First, each hit is multiplied by a random number between 85% and 100%. So a Pokémon is not automatically knocked out: there is a chance it survives and knocks out your Pokémon the next turn. Second, each attack has a 1 in 24 chance of a critical hit, which multiplies the damage by 1.5. Third, each move has a chance to hit its opponent, called accuracy. The trend in accuracy is that the stronger the move, the less accurate it is, as a form of game balance. Fourth, some moves have a secondary effect, such as flinch, which stops a Pokémon from making a move, burn, or paralysis. Fifth, there are speed ties: if two Pokémon have the same speed, the tie is resolved by a coin flip.
