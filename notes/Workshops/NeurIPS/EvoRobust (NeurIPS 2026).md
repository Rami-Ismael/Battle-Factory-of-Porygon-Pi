## What it is
A NeurIPS 2026 workshop on **diversity-driven / self-evolving search** for robust AI — evolutionary methods, quality-diversity (QD), and diversity as a mechanism for robustness. As of 2026-08-14 the full program/papers are not yet public.

## Relevance to my search problem — **MEDIUM-HIGH (surprisingly on-point)**
This one directly touches two things in my hub. (1) **Quality-Diversity** is the formal answer to my open question about *creativity / off-meta teams* — QD explicitly returns a diverse *set* of high-performing solutions instead of one optimum, which is exactly "many viable, distinct teams" rather than "the single best team." (2) Diversity-driven search is a legitimate way to explore a huge team space without brute force. Weakness: it's a heuristic/metaheuristic lens, not the exact CP/ILP framing — a complement, not a replacement.

## Papers that could interest me
1. **Quality-Diversity Algorithms Can Provably Be Helpful for Optimization** — [arXiv 2401.10539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10539). Theory that QD *helps* optimization, not just diversity for its own sake — justifies using it to find strong-and-varied teams.
2. **DEI: Diversity in Evolutionary Inference for Quality-Diversity Search** — [arXiv 2605.27130](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27130). Recent QD-search method; candidate technique for enumerating diverse team archetypes.
