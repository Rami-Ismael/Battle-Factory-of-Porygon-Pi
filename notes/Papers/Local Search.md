 - [ ] How to do local search in pokemon 

- “Local” means you search **around your current team**, keeping most of it intact. You decide what changes count as nearby.

|Edit|What remains fixed|
|---|---|
|Replace one move|Everything except that move|
|Change an item or EV spread|Other set choices and teammates|
|Replace one Pokémon with a complete legal set|The other five Pokémon|
|Replace two Pokémon together|A four-Pokémon core|
