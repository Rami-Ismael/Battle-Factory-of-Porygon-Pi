4. S. Jentzsch and K. Kersting, “ChatGPT is fun, but it is not funny! Humor is still challenging Large Language Models,” in *Proceedings of the 13th Workshop on Computational Approaches to Subjectivity, Sentiment, & Social Media Analysis (WASSA)*, Toronto, Canada, Jul. 2023, pp. 325–340, doi: [10.18653/v1/2023.wassa-1.29](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2023.wassa-1.29). [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.wassa-1.29/) · [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2023.wassa-1.29.pdf).

   **Finding:** More than 90% of 1,008 generated jokes repeated the same 25 jokes—not five. This result describes the ChatGPT version and prompting setup evaluated in the 2023 study.

   **Relevance to this project:** Evidence that generating many LLM outputs can yield a repetitive candidate pool. It motivates measuring duplicate rates and diversity in Pokémon team proposals; it does not directly establish that current LLMs fail at Pokémon team construction or that diffusion produces greater diversity.
