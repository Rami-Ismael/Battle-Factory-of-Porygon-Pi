What does this illustration leave out?

The toy uses observed positive improvement and normalizes it across this batch.

u(y) = max(y − τ, 0)
toy weightᵢ = u(yᵢ) / Σⱼ u(yⱼ)

Expected improvement is the conditional expectation of that utility. The paper's target also includes a reference prior. Its divergence-based objectives can include proposal-density importance weights and a density penalty. The bars omit these terms. Most experiments omit proposal-density importance weights, but that does not make these bars the complete GenBO loss.

Use Section 3.2, especially Equations 20 and 21, to identify the objective you would actually implement.

Try both leader cases at the default settings. Then explain why CEM's weights stay fixed while the improvement weights change. Afterward, read Section 3.2 to separate the toy from the full objective.
