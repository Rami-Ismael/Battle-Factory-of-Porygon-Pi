## Evidence for the opposite

No source found that says rankings hold steady across smooth and rough functions. Two partial counterweights sit inside the sources above. Pelikan, Section 4.5: "GA with uniform crossover performs better than GA with two-point crossover and UMDA regardless of k". Li et al., Discussion: "all MLDE strategies exceeded or at least matched DE performance across 16 landscapes". So some pairs keep their order while others flip, and the safe wording is "can change", not "decides".
