1. Pest Control has two groups of starting points, and their scores sit far apart

To ask whether one step is big, I divide it by how far scores usually sit from their average. That distance is the ruler. There are two ways to take it.

Hill-climbed starting pointsaverage score 14.2

one step moves a score 0.32
Ruler B: one group alone, 0.88
Ruler A: both groups together, 2.33

Ruler A, both groups lumped together

Scores sit 2.33 from the average, mostly because the two groups are far apart. One step is 0.32 ÷ 2.33 = 0.14 of the ruler. The step looks tiny.

Ruler B, each group on its own

Inside one group, scores sit 0.88 or 1.05 from that group's average. One step is 0.37 or 0.32 of the ruler. The same step looks more than twice as big.
