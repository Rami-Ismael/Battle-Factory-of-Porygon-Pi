Where this fits in your research

For counter-team search, you could explore: choose a target performance, generate legal team candidates, evaluate against a fixed opponent setup, and learn from measured results. Whether this saves evaluations is an experiment to run, not an established result for VGC.

What the paper actually implements

Five diffusion models with different initializations estimate uncertainty. For each target, Equation 7 takes the variance across models of their expected sample norms. This is not a separate forward MLP with Monte Carlo dropout. The reported 15% conditioning dropout supports classifier-free guidance.
