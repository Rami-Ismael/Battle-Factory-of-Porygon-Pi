Spotting a bad sample by asking slightly different models

An already-trained diffusion model, a little Bayesian doubt added to its last layer, and one question: if the model were slightly different, would this sample come out the same? Press play, or drag the slider.

    From the paper: post-hoc last-layer Laplace with a diagonal empirical-Fisher approximation; the same starting noise for all variants; M = 5 by default; CLIP features; uncertainty = entropy of a diagonal Gaussian fitted to the variants' features, with σ² = 0.001; 12,000 ImageNet samples per run, keeping the lowest-uncertainty 6,000–11,000 and scoring them with FID (lower is better). The FID numbers shown are at 10,000 kept. Models: ADM, U-ViT and a latent flow-matching model.

    Illustrative: the pictures are abstract stand-ins for generated images, and the 2-D feature plot is a sketch of a 512-number space. The last chapter is my reading of what would transfer to a masked diffusion model over teams; the paper does not test that.
