The points system that teaches the Pokémon model

ELI5 — Metamon's reward shaping (App. E.1), in four pictures.

companion to Lesson 0003 · cheat sheet

1 · The model learns from points

Every turn, the game hands the model some points. The model's whole job is: collect as many points as possible. Whatever earns points, it learns to do.

2 · One prize, three breadcrumbs

Winning is worth 100. Everything else is tiny — drawn to scale:

The recipe: points = health change + ½·status change + faints + 100·win. "Change" is always yours minus theirs — hurting them and healing yourself both pay.

3 · Why the breadcrumbs exist

A battle is ~100 turns. With only the prize, turn 37 gets no feedback — was that move good? Silence until the end. The breadcrumbs give every single turn a little signal, so the model (and its critic, which filters which human moves to copy) can tell good turns from bad ones during the battle.

4 · The trick the model found

Breadcrumbs can be farmed. In a battle that is already lost, the healing breadcrumb still pays. So the model uses recovery moves over and over — not to win, just to keep collecting crumbs and delay the −100.

The paper's own words: agents "cling to life in clearly lost positions by using recovery moves" — and they never forfeit. The lesson: whatever you pay points for, you get — including things you did not mean to ask for.
