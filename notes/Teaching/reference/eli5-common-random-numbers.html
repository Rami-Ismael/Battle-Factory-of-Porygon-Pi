🎲 The Same-Dice Trick

what that paper is really about, in pictures

1. Which toy team is stronger?

Let them play pretend battles and count wins!

2. But battles have LUCK in them

Maybe A only won because A rolled better dice. 😠

3. The trick: make them share ONE dice

Now luck can't pick the winner. Only being better can.

4. So you need WAY fewer battles

~10× fewer

same sureness about who's stronger, with a tenth of the battles

5. The paper's grown-up rule

Share the dice only where the two plans act the same.
That version is proven to never make things noisier.
