01 / Why a prior?

A plausible place to search

The diffusion model learns from past designs, with higher-scoring observations weighted more heavily. It provides the pθ(x) term that keeps proposed designs near learned support.

02 / Why uncertainty?

Room to discover

An ensemble proxy estimates score and disagreement. Its upper-confidence score, μ(x) + γσ(x), rewards promising and uncertain designs.

03 / Why sampling?

More than one mode

Fine-tuning a diffusion sampler toward the target distribution can yield candidates in multiple promising regions. Local search and filtering then focus the limited evaluation batch.

For this project

The paper studies continuous high-dimensional designs. Using this idea for Pokémon teams would require a legal structured representation and an evaluation pipeline; this page illustrates the optimization logic, not a validated team-search method.
