Bulbapedia is a fan wiki, so its text is secondary, but its screenshots show the games directly. "My reading" marks what I saw in an image and the page doesn't state.

## Not useful as an exterior

- **Generation IV page's main image:** my reading is glass panels and an entrance area, not a whole building: [Battle_Factory_IV.png](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/4/40/Battle_Factory_IV.png) 🟢.
- **Pokémon Masters EX:** both pages say artwork of the Battle Factory appears in the Factory Heads' mindscapes. Both images I opened show indoor scenes: [Noland](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/b/b8/Noland_Mindscape.png) 🟢 and [Thorton](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/8/83/Thorton_Mindscape.png) 🟢.

## Which games have it

- **Emerald:** it's in the Hoenn Battle Frontier ([Generation III page](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Battle_Factory_(Generation_III)) 🟢).
- **Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver:** it's in the Generation IV Battle Frontier, in its northeast corner ([Generation IV page](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Battle_Factory_(Generation_IV)) 🟢). The Sinnoh and Johto Frontiers are "identical in facilities and appearance" ([Generation IV Frontier page](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Battle_Frontier_(Generation_IV)) 🟢), so these three games share one look.
- **Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire:** the Battle Resort is where Emerald's Battle Frontier was ([Generation III Frontier page](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Battle_Frontier_(Generation_III)) 🟢). I didn't find a Battle Factory building there.
- **Other games:** I didn't check them. The pages' core-game coverage stops at these games, which suggests no later building, but that isn't proof.
