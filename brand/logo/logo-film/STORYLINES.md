# Storylines: how the Battle Factory of Porygon-Pi logo was made

Source: the nine construction boards in `brand/logo/0*/board*.png`, the proof sheets `brand/logo/proof*.png`, the finals in `brand/logo/final/`, and the 2026-10-30 decision (04b picked; 03 six tiles, 05 hexagon, 06 polygon bird, 04c and 04d short legs rejected; short legs read as bunting). Directions 01 and 02 have no recorded reason, so the film shows them cut without one.

Assumptions for all three: 1080 × 1350 (the 4:5 feed shape of the first video), 25 to 35 s, music only if HeyGen is connected (otherwise silent), no voiceover.

## A. Nine tries, one π (recommended: a stranger follows a contest with a winner)

- **Device and form:** one chosen out of many, as a split screen that collapses to one.
- **What the viewer sees:** all nine real construction boards play at once in a 3 × 3 grid. They drop out one by one, each with the reason it lost, until 04b fills the frame and passes the last test: it still reads at 16 px in a browser tab.
- **First 2 s:** the 3 × 3 wall of boards is already on screen, with "9 logos. 1 survives." over it.
- **Beats:** wall of nine → 01 and 02 go dark → hexagon, bird and six tiles cut with their reasons → three faceted πs left → short legs "read as bunting", no feet "reads 77" → 04b fills the frame and its grid lifts away → shrink test 512 → 32 → 16 px → the colour mark lands as the end card.
- **Look:** the boards' own paper white and grey construction lines, ink #111 type, accent only on the survivor's pink #E8607F and blue #2F8FC9.
- **Sound:** a tense, minimal pulse that drops out on each cut and opens on the survivor; silent without HeyGen.

## B. Zoom into the favicon

- **Device and form:** the result first, as an endless zoom.
- **What the viewer sees:** a browser tab with the 16 px favicon. The camera dives in until the pixels become the 25 triangles, then into one triangle until it is a cell of the lattice, and finally into the line of `build.mjs` that placed it.
- **First 2 s:** a real browser tab row with the tiny pink and blue icon, and the camera already pushing in.
- **Beats:** tab → 32 px icon → full mark → one facet → the triangular grid → `D(a, b)` in the build script → pull all the way back out to the tab.
- **Look:** neutral browser chrome, then the mark's paper ground, then the code's dark editor; pink and blue carry across every world.
- **Sound:** a rising, airy bed that resolves on the pull back out; silent without HeyGen.

## C. Three words, one mark (the bold one)

- **Device and form:** type carries the argument, with match cuts.
- **What the viewer sees:** giant words take the frame one at a time: "Battle Factory", "Porygon", "π". Each word's shape becomes the next picture. The π glyph holds while the world behind it changes from a serif letter to faceted triangles to the final colour mark.
- **First 2 s:** "BATTLE FACTORY" fills the frame edge to edge in ink on paper.
- **Beats:** Battle Factory (rent a team) → Porygon (made of polygons) → π (the symbol for a policy) → the serif π match-cuts to the faceted π → colour floods it → the name lands as the end card.
- **Look:** an oversized display grotesque in black on #F3F1EC, colour fields in pink and blue that cut on the beat.
- **Sound:** cut on the beat of a punchy 120 bpm loop; silent without HeyGen.
