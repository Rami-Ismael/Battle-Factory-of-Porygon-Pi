# Neural combinatorial optimization — relevance grades

Checked 2026-09-07. These grades measure usefulness to the current VGC project, not scientific quality. **HIGH:** directly informs an active implementation decision with a close match in assumptions. **MED:** a concrete transferable design or evaluation idea worth selective reading. **LOW:** background or a deferred direction with substantial mismatch. No paper here establishes efficiency for expensive, noisy battle evaluation.

Current scope comes from [[Todo Section]], [[Metrics for Diversity]], [[Diffusion as candidate proposer in black-box optimization over structured inputs]], and the latest owner decisions in [[SPIGM 2026 — full-paper reassessment]]: legal generation, training-pool comparisons, structural diversity and model-based search; reward integration is deferred and diffusion training is not an established bottleneck. Proposed Pokémon transfers below are our judgments, not the papers' experimental findings.

| Paper | Grade | Reason and selective reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neural Large Neighborhood Search for the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem | **MED** | Removes parts of a solution and completes it with a learned repair policy. Concrete experiment: preserve a promising team core and regenerate selected fields or members. The routing policy, legality handling and acceptance rule need adaptation; noisy battle comparisons are not addressed. Read §§3.1 and 3.3 first; defer reinforcement-learning repair training. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1911.09539v2). |
| DIFUSCO: Graph-based Diffusion Solvers for Combinatorial Optimization | **LOW**, unchanged | Learns graph decisions from solver-labeled solutions and uses problem-specific decoding and search. Useful foundational material, already captured in [[DIFUSCO - reading guide]], but no new solution to current generator evaluation or battle allocation. Skim §3.5 and §4.2 only if those design choices remain unclear. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.08224). |
| A Diffusion Model Framework for Unsupervised Neural Combinatorial Optimization (DiffUCO) | **LOW**, previously MED | Removes optimal-solution supervision using energy-based diffusion training. Its demonstrated QUBO formulations allow tractable conditional expected energies and decoding; battle estimates do not automatically provide these. Revisit §§2–4 if objective-driven generator training becomes active. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01661v3), [ICML 2024 proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/sanokowski24a.html). |
| Tackling Prevalent Conditions in Unsupervised Combinatorial Optimization | **LOW** | Derives expected objectives and incremental derandomization for conditions such as cardinality and covering. Potentially useful for a future explicitly structured surrogate objective; there is no demonstrated tractable equivalent for battle win rate, or established need to replace current feasibility handling. Skip now. [Full paper, §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.08424v2), [ICML 2024 proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bu24b.html). |
| Learning the Travelling Salesperson Problem Requires Rethinking Generalization | **MED** | Separates learned-model performance from improvements supplied by decoding/search and studies distribution shifts. Useful for comparing team generators with equal sampling/search budgets and held-out structures. TSP-size extrapolation does not directly match fixed six-member teams, and the paper does not solve battle noise. Read §§3.4, 5.5 and Appendix C. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.07054), [CP 2021 proceedings](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.CP.2021.33). |
| A Denoising Diffusion-Based Evolutionary Algorithm Framework: Application to the Maximum Independent Set Problem | **MED**, implementation deferred | Diffusion initializes populations and generates offspring conditioned on two parents. This offers a specific alternative to whole-population elite refitting: generate team offspring from two strong parents. Its learned crossover uses integer-programming expert examples and exact graph fitness, so it is not a ready-made noisy team optimizer. Read §§3–4 for architecture and initialization/crossover ablations. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08627v1), [published chapter](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-21477-5_1). LOD 2025, proceedings published in 2026. |

Reading order within MED: **Neural Large Neighborhood Search → Learning TSP generalization sections → diffusion evolutionary framework**. The first supplies a candidate-generation experiment, the second sharpens evaluation, and the third is a more demanding architecture option. None justifies implementing a new reward-trained model now.
