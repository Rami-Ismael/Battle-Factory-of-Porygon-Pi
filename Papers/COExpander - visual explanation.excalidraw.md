Why this can help ^coe00153

Fixed choices reduce ambiguity in the next global prediction.
A batch can settle many variables between neural calls.
The paper evaluates 6 problems on 29 benchmarks (50-10K nodes):
independent set, clique, vertex cover, cut, TSP and asymmetric TSP. ^coe00154

What the method does not guarantee ^coe00156

Greedy commitments can be wrong: there is no optimality guarantee.
Training requires labeled solutions; constraints need specific rules.
Reported gains depend on the task, inference budget and local search.
More complex constraints remain a limitation (e.g., vehicle capacity). ^coe00157
