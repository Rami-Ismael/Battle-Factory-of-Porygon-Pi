## Destination

One finished experiment. A masked or fully bidirectional text diffusion model, chosen on a laptop benchmark, fine-tuned on Modal in two stages, uploaded to a public Hugging Face repository, then battled on the laptop, with a verdict against the Fast-dLLM v2 1.5B and Qwen2.5 3B block fine-tunes on every metric below. The map also holds the block-against-bidirectional swap test, and four pre-registrations written but not run. It ends when those are done, or when the owner stops at the gate.

## Notes

This map carries execution: its task tickets build and run things. Nothing launched while charting. Every Modal launch states its cost and the credit left, then waits for the owner's go-ahead. One job at a time.

**Where each step runs.** Only training and its probes run on Modal. When training ends: shut down the GPU, upload to a public repository under `Rami/` with a model card, confirm the SHA-256, download to the laptop, check the SHA-256 again. Text generation and battles run only on the laptop (M4 Pro, 24 GB), in a detached queue, behind any other session's jobs.

**Who does what.** Opus 5.5 runs this map, `pskill:recall` and `build-experiment`. GPT-6.1 Sol at high reasoning effort runs the deep-research skill on research tickets, through `delegate_task`.

**How a research ticket closes.**
1. GPT-6.1 Sol researches and commits its findings to a throwaway `research/<name>` branch, pushed, with no PR.
2. A fresh Opus 5.5 agent tries to refute them from the full sources, without seeing any rewrite.
3. Opus rewrites what survives and adds a visual with `storyboard-figures`.
4. If the research found a good blog, a subagent checks whether it belongs in `Papers/Reading List.md`.

**Settled with the owner while charting.**
- The map ends at a finished experiment, not at a launch-ready plan.
- Step 1 is split around the prompt: candidate research, then the prompt visual counted with each candidate's tokenizer, then approval, then the laptop benchmark, then the prompt-only test.
- The refutation agent is a fresh Opus 5.5.
- Modal credit comes later from the dashboard, so Modal steps stay blocked and uncosted until then.
- Block is out as the base model, for budget reasons only: no one has shown it reaches good teams more slowly. The only block training allowed is the swap test.

**Metrics**, from `What metric do we care about.md`, with a baseline on every row:
- Mean squared error between the model's ask-versus-got line and the true diagonal.
- Win rate against the top 1-50 meta teams and against the 51-100 teams in Regulation M-B: wins ÷ battles, behaviour cloning on both sides, slots shuffled.
- Diversity and novelty, following `Metrics for Diversity.md`. Novelty means six species not seen before, against one named reference.
- Validity: the share of generated teams Showdown accepts.
- The share of Pokémon spending all 66 Stat Points. Training teams do this 99.3% of the time.
