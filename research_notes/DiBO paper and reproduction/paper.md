## What domain adaptation and supervision do

### Takeaway
The paper's domain adaptation already uses a scored improvement-pair corpus. Supervision reuses that corpus and changes which tokens are masked and optimized. Unscored-team ingestion is an extension, not a demonstrated paper feature.

### Cited Findings
- v3 §3.1 pp3-4 adds four tokenizer delimiters `|design-start|`, `|design-end|`, `|label-start|`, `|label-end|`. Prompt contains task semantics, output format and objective, scored design examples, and an improvement instruction. Response contains a design better than all prompt designs, not a score. A.3 pp14-15 shows concrete task examples. [v3 §3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S3.SS1).
- A.1 p13: sort labeled designs by score and take an evenly spaced pool of 500. A.2 p13 describes disjoint lower-score context and higher-score target pools with ratio 0.8:0.2, so no target design appears as context. Prompt examples are selected for similarity to the target. Similarity is an RBF kernel on normalized designs; discrete sequences use `map_to_logits()` followed by feature normalization; continuous tasks use normalized continuous features; bandwidth is median pairwise Euclidean distance over offline pool. Seven context designs are used in main experiments, §4.3 p6. [v3 A.1-A.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#A1.SS2).
- §3.1 Eq3 p4 reconstructs masked tokens over BOTH prompt and response. Sample t uniformly on [0,1], corrupt tokens using the diffusion masking process, weight masked-token negative log probabilities by 1/t. This learns prompt/design/label roles jointly. It is not a separate squared-error win-rate loss. [v3 §3.1 Eq3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S3.E3).
- §3.2 Eq4 p4 reuses the unified corpus; prompt q remains clean, response o is corrupted, and only masked response tokens receive weighted negative log probability loss. It supplies a binary improvement relation through corpus construction, without the quantitative improvement weighting used later in reinforcement learning. [v3 §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S3.SS2).
- A.3 p13 describes 10 instruction paraphrases, 8 training and 2 validation, generated by ChatGPT-5.2. All retain task/objective/format and four delimiters. [v3 A.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#A1.SS3).

### Inferences
- The best labeled example need not find a superior target: choose it AS a target and supply inferior nearby contexts. Paper construction does not require each point to serve as a prompt anchor. Tie handling and precise feasible-target sampling require released-code inspection.
- The paper defines the data as labeled pairs throughout. It gives no missing-label sentinel, score imputation, or unscored-only adaptation objective. For Pokémon, using unscored pastes for design-only denoising is plausible but an additional proposed method.
- Similarity construction, delimiter representation and joint-loss scope are conceptually separate decisions. The delimiter ablation keeps the pipeline and replaces boundaries with ordinary text; it does not test removal of similarity or joint prompt loss. Table 5 separately tests random context at the FULL pipeline endpoint, not a supervision-only result.

### Gaps
- Paper alone does not establish a precise target draw distribution, tie policy, implementation behavior for NaNs, or code separability. These belong in the code audit.

## Table 3 interpretation

### Takeaway
The requested pairs are confirmed in v3 Table 3 p8 and compare boundary representations after sequential domain adaptation then supervised fine-tuning. They are not supervision-only versus adaptation-plus-supervision.

### Cited Findings
All entries below are mean ± standard deviation across 8 random seeds, per v3 §4.3 p6. §4.5 p7 states ablations use the main evaluation protocol. Table 3 p8 explicitly describes the SFT column as sequential training through that stage. [v3 Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.T3).

| Boundary representation after adaptation then supervision | TF Bind 8 | Ant Morphology |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain-text boundaries | 0.918 ± 0.043 | 0.903 ± 0.022 |
| Delimiter-token boundaries | 0.939 ± 0.031 | 0.929 ± 0.017 |
| Delimiter-token minus plain-text mean | +0.021 | +0.026 |

- Plain-text boundary examples are `Designs:` and `Labels:`. Delimiter boundary examples are the four tokens listed above. The comparison changes boundary representation, not training-stage presence. [v3 §4.5/Table 3 p8](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.T3).
- §4.1 p5 defines score as best of 128 proposed candidates under task oracle, normalized `(y - y_min)/(y_max - y_min)` using extrema over the full unobserved dataset. It is neither mean candidate quality nor a Pokémon win rate. TF Bind 8 optimizes length-8 DNA binding to SIX6_REF_R1; Ant optimizes 60-dimensional morphology. [v3 §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.SS1).
- §4.3 p6 says inference independently resamples context per candidate and greedily fills the masked response in ONE forward pass, rejects duplicates, and continues until 128 unique valid candidates. This materially specifies what a benchmark reproduction must score. [v3 §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.SS3).

### Inferences
- TF Bind 8 improvement 0.021 is smaller than either SD; one-SD intervals [0.875,0.961] and [0.908,0.970] overlap widely.
- Ant improvement 0.026 exceeds either individual SD but intervals [0.881,0.925] and [0.912,0.946] overlap. Quadrature SD of a difference under independence is sqrt(0.022²+0.017²)=0.0278, exceeding the 0.026 difference. TF Bind 8 analog sqrt(0.043²+0.031²)=0.0530.
- Approximate independent-seed standard errors of mean differences with n=8 are 0.0187 for TF Bind 8 and 0.00983 for Ant. Corresponding ratios are about 1.12 and 2.64. These are conditional calculations, not paper hypothesis tests. Ant may resolve under an unpaired test; overlapping SD intervals do NOT establish non-significance. Paired per-seed differences could behave differently.

### Gaps
- Specific eight seed values, per-seed results, covariance/paired-seed design and a predeclared significance test are absent from the paper. Do not state either result is statistically significant or insignificant solely from ±SD.

## Pretraining audit and limits

### Takeaway
Appendix A.7 performs a behavioral ranking probe. It does not inspect the unavailable pretraining corpus and cannot rule out contamination.

### Cited Findings
- v3 A.7/Table 12 p18: use PRETRAINED LLaDA-8B-Instruct before adaptation or post-training. For EACH task and EACH K in {2,3,4,5}, sample 500 groups of K designs from offline pool. Show rendered designs without labels, assign option letters, ask for highest-to-lowest ranking, and request only option-letter ordering. Enumerate all K! candidate answer permutations, compute model log probability for each, sort those answer scores, record the rank assigned to ground-truth oracle-label ordering, and average those ranks. [v3 A.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#A1.SS7).
- The random expected rank is `(K!+1)/2`, giving 1.5, 3.5, 12.5, 60.5. Table12 results: Ant 1.55/3.39/11.77/64.40; D'Kitty 1.55/3.30/11.84/60.90; TF Bind8 1.56/3.43/11.62/61.96; TF Bind10 1.58/3.29/11.62/63.00; RNA-A 1.59/3.49/11.85/62.94; RNA-B 1.56/3.50/11.51/63.54; RNA-C 1.54/3.52/11.58/64.44. [v3 Table12 p18](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#A1.T12).
- Authors explicitly say LLaDA pretraining corpus is unavailable, preventing exact dataset-level overlap audit. They interpret near-random ranking performance as suggesting no meaningful prior design-label relationship knowledge. [v3 A.7 p18](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#A1.SS7).

### Inferences
- Across seven tasks there are 7×4×500=14,000 groups and 7×500×(2!+3!+4!+5!)=532,000 answer likelihood evaluations before any batching. This calculation is not reported compute usage.
- Failure to express knowledge through this specific ranking prompt does not prove lack of memorization, familiarity with benchmark descriptions or biological priors. It audits observable ranking behavior only.

### Gaps
- No confidence intervals, repeated probe seeds, log-probability estimator details for diffusion scoring, or tie-breaking rule is provided here. Whether audit scripts are released requires code inspection.

## Hardware and cost anchors

### Takeaway
The paper reports one TF Bind 8 full-pipeline duration, not an itemized cost measurement. Ant duration and supervision-only pair duration cannot be read directly from it.

### Cited Findings
- v3 §4.3 pp5-6: initialize LLaDA-8B-Instruct; single NVIDIA H100 80GB; bf16; bitsandbytes PagedAdamW8bit; batch1; accumulation16 discrete /8 continuous; each stage 100-step linear warmup then constant rate. Domain adaptation 1024 discrete /2048 continuous optimizer steps; supervision1024 all; reinforcement128 all. DA/SFT/RL sample counts respectively16384/16384/2048 discrete,16384/8192/1024 continuous. Discrete DA and SFT rates2e-5; continuous DA1e-5, SFT2e-5; reinforcement1e-6. [v3 §4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.SS3).
- Same section reports entire TF Bind8 pipeline can complete within1.5 hours. It supplies no Ant duration, per-stage timing, stage checkpoint storage cost, dollar price, or laptop timing. [v3 §4.3 p6](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17919v3#S4.SS3).

### Inferences
- Short length-8 output and simple discrete task favor TF Bind8 for cheap method replication; Ant has 60 real-valued dimensions and far longer rendered examples.
- Even equal-step proportional estimates need assumptions because joint prompt loss, response-only loss, reinforcement loss, evaluation, and checkpointing have different costs.

### Gaps
- Paper does not establish actual repeat-run GPU-hours, Ant GPU-hours, per-arm timing, laptop throughput, or performance for Pokémon teams. No training or generation was run for these notes.
