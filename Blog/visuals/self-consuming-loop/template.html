The papers behind this strategy

For the team generator's self-training loop

That loop retrains the generator on its own best teams, selected by battles: a curated self-consuming loop. It stopped at round 2 on a concentration alarm (2026-08-29).

1 · Keep the real teams inRefit on the meta teams plus the selected teams every round, never on selected teams alone. The cheapest fix and the best supported.
2 · Treat battles as curationThe loop optimises whatever the battles reward and amplifies their errors. Re-battle a team before admitting it: 24-battle labels were winner's-cursed (0.79 → 0.57).
3 · Try Neon onceFine-tune on ~1k of the generator's own teams, then move the weights the other way. Measure legality, distance to the meta teams and win rate.

This is a toy: a five-component Gaussian mixture refitted to 120 samples per round, with a sampler that slightly favours typical outputs (the papers' sampling bias). It shows the mechanisms the papers analyse, not their experiments. The reward is a smooth stand-in for win rate. Full reference with grades: docs/self-consuming-loop-fixes.md in the code repository.
