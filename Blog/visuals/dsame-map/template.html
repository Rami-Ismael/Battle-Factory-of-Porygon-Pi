Experiment design · deep-surrogate MAP-Elites vs the existing search loop · pre-registered 2026-09-30

Elites on a map

The project's search loop keeps one list of its best teams. MAP-Elites keeps a map instead: one champion per kind of team, sorted by how offensive the team is and how long its battles run. A neural surrogate does the map's searching, so no battle is spent during its 8,000-child search. Same starting data and same battle budget as the existing loop; the results fill in below as the experiment runs.
