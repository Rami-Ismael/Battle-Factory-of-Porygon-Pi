## Initial reading-list decision — superseded where noted

**Add no papers from this workshop at present.** The [official accepted list](https://sites.google.com/view/eimlicml2026/accepted-papers_1) contains 79 papers: 4 oral papers, 13 spotlights, and 62 posters. All 79 have a screening record below. Of these, 59 were excluded on current project fit and 20 lack enough accessible primary material to establish a worthwhile addition. This is a relevance screen with selective deeper inspection, **not 79 full-paper reviews**. “Skip” does not mean scientifically bad; “unverified” is an access/evidence limitation.

The [ICML workshop page](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/workshop/54075) supplied additional official presentation abstracts. OpenReview requested challenge verification for several manuscripts. Accessible arXiv papers, author repositories, and official abstracts were used where available; author announcements establish identity but do not establish scientific quality. The evidence column states what was actually inspected. Papers 14 and 57 have related or differently titled author manuscripts; their version caveats remain explicit below.

## What a paper needed to contribute

The latest owner decisions in [[Todo Section]] (2026-09-06) prioritize categorical discrete team generation: real teams versus real teams plus mutations, classifier-free guidance versus legality/win rate/diversity, and diversity across cross-entropy-method generations, with original-data anchoring as a possible follow-up. The verifier already handles legality. A separate active strand is battle-policy reproduction and evaluation in [[VGC-Bench Towards Mastering Diverse Team Strategies in Competitive Pokémon]].

A reading-list addition needed a concrete method or diagnostic for one of those questions, supported by enough primary evidence to assess its assumptions and limitations. Broad similarity in terminology was insufficient. The latest [[Reading List]] decisions retire molecular optimization, continuous/noise-space search, information-seeking acquisition as an end in itself, and partial-team reward assumptions. Reward integration is deferred; a deferred paper still needs a plausible implementation connection. These decisions supersede older grades in Teaching/RESOURCES.md.

## Closest candidates and why they did not pass

- **1 — Diffusion as Prior Construction:** the official abstract describes mixing beliefs between communicating agents before Bayesian correction. This is not the categorical generative-diffusion method used to propose teams. A centrally controlled VGC policy also does not require communication between independently informed teammates. [Official abstract](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/84057).
- **25 — Space-sampled Value Decay:** the best adjacent battle-policy lead. It deliberately decays critic values to adapt to drift, but the paper reports weaker results in high-dimensional Ant and relies on sampling suitable states/actions. There is no demonstrated need for this intervention in the current VGC policy. Revisit only after measuring an adaptation problem. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.11797v1).
- **42 — In-Context Learning for Latent Space Bayesian Optimization:** a tested surrogate approach, but its molecular VAE latent search belongs to a direction the owner already closed. It does not establish a categorical diffusion update. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09664).
- **47 — Indirect Query Bayesian Optimization:** observes conditional averages over inputs that cannot be queried directly. Showdown instead returns noisy outcomes for the particular complete team selected. Repeating that team's battles does not turn the task into this paper's indirect-query setting. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.13559).
- **62 — Distributional Energy-Based Models:** separates learned quality from exact constraint checking and includes useful scorer-confounding tests. That is a reasonable pattern, but the project already separates legality from battle outcomes. The paper supplies no evidence that its LLM verifier improves noisy team selection or CEM diversity enough to justify another reading. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18871).
- **71 — Advantage Sign Robustness:** has a concrete mechanism and ablations for learned-reward policy gradients. Its local reward-head certificate is not a certificate of true reward correctness, and CEM elite refitting is a different update. Reward work being planned for later does not by itself establish a use for this particular RLHF method. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.02986).
