# Species-draw diversity: Pokémon-by-Pokémon order and temperature on species

Pre-registered 2026-11-14, before any sampling. Parent map: [Diversity map: why does the default model write half the line-ups and fewer builds?](https://github.com/Rami-Ismael/Battle-Factory-of-Porygon-Pi/issues/263). Mode: **confirmatory** for the default model alone, because the plan, the control and the rules are fixed here before any arm is sampled.

## Why these two arms

In species-first order the six species are the first six passes, so a team's line-up is fixed before any of its sets is visible. A change to how set fields are drawn cannot add line-ups. Two levers act on the species draws themselves:

- **Pokémon-by-Pokémon order** changes what a species draw sees. Pokémon 2's species is drawn after Pokémon 1's whole set, so it can respond to that set instead of to five bare species.
- **Temperature on species only** changes how a species is drawn. Dividing the species logits by a temperature above 1 flattens the species odds and leaves every set field at temperature 1.

The owner chose both on 2026-11-14 after the reading on decoding order and diversity: "pre-register these two as a ticket under the diversity map #263". The temperatures 1.25 and 1.5 are the agent's suggestion.

### Measures and the question

This is a Pareto question: each arm trades line-ups against win rate.

- **Line-up axis**, the primary measure: distinct line-ups among the 1,088 teams of one seed on asks 1/24 to 17/24, 64 per ask. This is the map's measure. The control scores 461, 475 and 471 for seeds 1, 2 and 3, recounted on 2026-11-14 from the stored grid with `team_diversity.slots_from_paste`. Win-rate-matched training teams score 902 to 922.
- **Win-rate axis**: the pooled win rate of all 4,416 teams, as on issue #297. The control's is 0.515.
- **The previous frontier** on this model is the control's single point. Guidance 1 and 4 from the guidance sweep, issue #265, would add line-up points without win rates. The older levers above ran on other models and are context, not frontier.
- **Where the new curve must move**: to the right of the control by at least 10% on every seed, and no more than 0.01 below it. The two temperatures trace the temperature arm's curve; one order point is enough for the order arm.
- **Reported beside**: first-try legality; ask-versus-got error, the mean squared gap between got and ask on asks 1/24 to 17/24, 0.0053 for the control; the ask-response slope, 0.502 for the control; distinct line-ups within one ask on asks 13/24 to 17/24, 44 to 61 for the control; the rest of the map's measurement list, which includes builds per species, distinct species, Novelty and Nearest training distance; and the share of teams that copy a training team.

Within one ask, distinct line-ups saturate at 64 of 64 on asks 1/24 to 4/24 for the control, so the within-ask count is reported only on the high asks and is not the primary measure.

### Stages and stopping rule

1. **Sampling only, no battles.** Each arm samples its 4,416 teams. An arm whose line-ups do not rise by at least 10% on all three seeds stops here and is reported as failing the claim. This stage costs minutes of laptop time and no battles.
2. **Battles** for each arm that passes stage 1: its 216,384 battles, paired with the control by sampling seed and ask.

## Noise floor and budget

- **Line-ups**: the control's three seeds span 461 to 475. The 10% bar, about 47 line-ups, is more than three times that spread.
- **Win rate**: one arm has 216,384 battles, so battle noise alone gives a standard error of about 0.0011 on the pooled win rate. A paired difference of 0.01 needs about 3.9 / 0.01² = 39,000 battles per arm, so the budget detects the −0.01 bound from battle noise. Sampling seeds add variance that one checkpoint cannot separate from the arm, so the interval is taken over the three seed differences, and the run is confirmatory for this checkpoint only.
- **Total**: at most 649,152 battles if all three arms pass stage 1, run on the laptop behind the current queue.
