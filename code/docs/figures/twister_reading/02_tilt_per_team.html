Per team: how much more likely the sampler made it than the trained model's own per-pass distributions, guidance 2, asks 13/24 to 17/24

For each drawn field, the sampler removes probability the trained model gave (the [MASK] value, the empty first move, values the legal Pokémon construction tables forbid given fields drawn earlier in the same pass, and the lookahead) and renormalises. Summed -log(kept share) over the 84 draws = the log of TWISTER's correction weight for that team. Box: 25th to 75th percentile; whiskers: 5th and 95th. 192 teams per ask, the grid's own teams redrawn with their seeds and checked identical.
