The model is not one joint distribution: the same held-out team scores 18.2 nats higher species-first

Log-likelihood of 1,024 held-out teams in species-first order minus moves-first order, by field type

Reused from the Two Temperatures reading (2026-11-14), order_consistency.py. One joint distribution would give 0 in every row. Species-first total minus moves-first total: 18.2 nats per team [17.7, 18.8] on the per-field checkpoint and 12.3 [11.9, 12.7] on the linear one. Species score better when drawn after their moves; moves score far worse when drawn before their species, and the second effect is larger.
