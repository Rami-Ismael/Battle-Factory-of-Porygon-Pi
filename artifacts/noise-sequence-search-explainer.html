Why the paragraph lists limitations

These are method-dependent tradeoffs, not claims about every method equally.

01

Incomplete images need a proxy

To choose a partial path early, a method may estimate its eventual reward before the final sample exists. That estimate can be wrong.

02

Adaptive search complicates batching

The next candidates depend on earlier scores or branches, making large fixed batches less convenient.

03

SMC calls the reward often

As described here, FKS and DAS use SDE sampling and many score evaluations, so cheap reward models are more practical.

Plain English

Guiding a stochastic trajectory can work well, but decisions along the way add scoring and coordination costs.
