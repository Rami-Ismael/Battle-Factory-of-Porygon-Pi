One record, one count

Win rates use a 0–1 scale: 0.38 means 38%. New labels use the top-50 membership-weighted opponent win rate, with BC play on both sides, one battle per opponent, and self-matches skipped. Draws count as non-wins. Existing labels are preserved. These are initial estimates, not high-confidence rankings. Halfway values round up: 0.125 → 0.13.
