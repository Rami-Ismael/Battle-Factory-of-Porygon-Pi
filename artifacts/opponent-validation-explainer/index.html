Only now open ranks 51 to 100.

Use their opponent descriptions in one final prompted evaluation and measure the locked models' battle results. If we tune settings after seeing those results, these ranks have become another validation set.

Why not choose settings from training results?

That rewards doing well on familiar opponents. We want to know whether the model can use a new opponent description to generate a team that beats it.
