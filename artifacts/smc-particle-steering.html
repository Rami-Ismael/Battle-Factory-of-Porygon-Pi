Propose

The stochastic sampler supplies new candidate states. The animation uses toy random steps; it does not reproduce a diffusion model.

Weight and resample

An intermediate potential estimates which partial paths are promising. Importance-weighted resampling duplicates some paths and may remove others.

Why it costs more

Several particles need repeated potential evaluations. In its continuous-sampling comparison, the paper says FKS and DAS use SDE-based sampling and favor cheap reward models in practice.
